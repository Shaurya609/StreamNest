# StreamNest 0.15.1

Android and Windows anime searches now discover Japanese animated series beyond the saved catalog through explicit IMDb metadata. Includes the broader discovery fix for missing titles such as Black Clover and Dragon Ball Super. Catalog entries do not guarantee available episodes or playable sources.

The signed catalog feed includes Black Clover, Dragon Ball Super and Dragon Ball Super: Beerus. Existing settings and pairing use the normal update workflow.
